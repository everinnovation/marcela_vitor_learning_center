{% blocktrans %}Our Unique Approach{% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "What makes Marcela Vitor Learning Center truly special is our commitment to providing Massachusetts' only Portuguese-English bilingual education program with Montessori-inspired principles. {% trans "Bilingual Immersion" %}

{% trans "Our educators are fluent in both Portuguese and English, providing a natural language-learning environment through daily activities, stories, songs, and conversations. Children develop bilingual skills without formal instruction, absorbing vocabulary, pronunciation, and cultural nuances organically." %}

{% trans "Montessori-Inspired Learning" %}

{% trans "We incorporate key Montessori principles including child-led learning, prepared environments with hands-on materials, mixed-age groupings, uninterrupted work periods, and respect for each child's developmental timeline. These principles support independence, concentration, and intrinsic motivation." %}
